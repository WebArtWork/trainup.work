## 53. Training Plateaus

### EN: Short
A training plateau is a stretch of time when an ability stops improving even though you keep practicing. Plateaus are common, and the cause can be recovery, too little challenge, inconsistent measuring, or the slower progress that comes with experience.

### EN: Detailed
**First check that it is a plateau.** One disappointing session is not a plateau. Compare several weeks under similar conditions: the same exercise, load, range and effort. Changes in equipment, sleep or technique can make your numbers look worse even when your fitness is not falling. Record enough detail to tell a real trend from day-to-day ups and downs.

**Check the basics.** Did you finish your sessions? Did the resistance stay challenging, and did you recover between workouts? Was the volume right for you? Sometimes the workload is too low, and sometimes it is higher than it needs to be. A lift can also stall because you haven’t practiced that movement regularly, while your muscles are still adapting.

**Make one useful change.** If you finish your repetitions easily, add a little load or use double progression. If fatigue is building and performance is dropping, cut back on hard sets for a while or rearrange your schedule. If one movement has stopped improving, try a technique change or a closely related variation and keep the rest of your program as it is.

**Slower gains over time.** Beginners often improve fast, partly because they are learning the movements. Experienced lifters progress more slowly and in smaller steps. Muscle growth takes time to measure, so an unchanged scale or a single performance test does not prove that your training failed. Have a doctor check fatigue you can’t explain or other health symptoms that persist.

**Using TrainUp.** History shows your workouts, your average effort and a 4-week chart, which can help you spot a possible plateau. TrainUp never changes your plan based on fewer than 3 workouts, so one hard day does not trigger a new program. Make small adjustments and check again after several sessions.
