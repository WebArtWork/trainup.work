## 69. Training in a Gym: Etiquette and Safety

### EN: Short
Training safely in a gym means good technique plus care for shared equipment and the people around you. Clear etiquette lowers the risk of injury and makes equipment easier for everyone to use when it is busy.

### EN: Detailed
**Prepare each station.** Check the bench, rack, machine or attachment before you load it. Set seats and safety bars correctly, use collars where needed, and make sure plates are secured on both sides. Keep bags and loose accessories out of walkways. If you do not know how a machine works, ask the staff to show you.

**Share the space.** Put dumbbells and plates back, wipe equipment as the gym asks, and return cable attachments. Do not hold several machines at once at peak times. Ask politely whether you can work in, and agree on how to change the weight or seat safely before you share a station.

**Spotters and safeties.** For hard bench presses or squats, set the safety arms when you can, and use a spotter who knows what they are doing when you need one. Do not count on a stranger nearby noticing a failed rep. Practice setting up a rack with light loads before you lift heavy, and never step into someone else’s lifting path.

**Busy periods.** Have a backup for popular equipment: a dumbbell row can replace a cable row, and a goblet squat can replace a rack squat if the load still suits you. Your workout does not depend on getting one particular station. A rushed or crowded setup is still no reason to cut corners on technique or skip your warm-up.
