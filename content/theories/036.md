## 36. Vertical Pushing

### EN: Short
Vertical pushing drives resistance overhead, as in a dumbbell shoulder press, an overhead barbell press or a pike push-up. It trains shoulder and arm strength and needs your shoulder blades, trunk and hips to work together.

### EN: Detailed
**Overhead mechanics.** Start from a stable standing or seated base. Keep your ribs roughly stacked over your pelvis, brace your trunk, and press up along a comfortable path so your arms finish overhead without a big backward lean. Your shoulder blades normally rotate upward as your arms rise, and trying to pin them down for the whole lift can get in the way of that.

**Mobility and individual differences.** Reaching overhead takes enough shoulder movement and usually some help from the upper back. Shoulder anatomy and comfortable grip angles differ from person to person. If a straight-bar press is uncomfortable, a neutral-grip dumbbell press, a landmine press or a shorter overhead range may suit you better. Sharp pain means stop, so don’t try to stretch through it.

**Bodyweight options.** A pike push-up puts your hips high, so your shoulders handle more of the load. An elevated pike push-up can be harder and needs more balance and room. For beginners, a light standing dumbbell press is often simpler and easier to adjust than a hard bodyweight variation.

**Common technique problems.** A big arch in the lower back, pressing the weight far in front of you, and rushing shaky reps usually mean the load is too heavy or your control is limited. Lower the weight, practice the path, and keep your legs out of a strict press. A push press is a separate exercise that adds leg drive on purpose.

**Using TrainUp.** Your plan only includes overhead exercises when your equipment and the ceiling height in your Profile leave enough room. It also matches exercises to your coordination. If sensitive shoulders or loading through your spine make standard overhead presses a poor fit, add a limitation for that body area in Profile and the plan picks less demanding options.
