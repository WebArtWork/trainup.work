## 117. Myth: You Can Burn Fat From One Body Part

### EN: Short
Training your abs doesn’t let you reliably choose where your body loses fat. Targeted exercises strengthen the muscles you train, while where fat sits and how it changes depend on wider biological and behavioral factors.

### EN: Detailed
**The common claim.** Hundreds of crunches are often sold as a way to lose belly fat, and inner-thigh exercises are advertised as a way to target fat there. The working muscles do get stronger or better at sustained effort. Using a muscle still doesn’t give you dependable control over the fat near it.

**What the research says.** Changes in body fat depend on energy balance, genetics, hormones and overall activity. Practical training advice shouldn’t promise precise fat loss from one area. The research isn’t completely settled: a small 2023 trial found more trunk-fat loss with one specific abdominal endurance protocol. That result doesn’t show that ordinary crunches reliably decide where any one person loses fat.

**What targeted training does.** Planks, curls, lunges and other exercises can improve the strength, endurance and function of the muscles they work. Combine resistance training with aerobic activity for broader health and fitness. How much body fat you lose isn’t a direct measure of how good your exercise is.

**Example.** If you want a stronger core, practice dead bugs, side planks and loaded carries. They build trunk strength and endurance whether or not your waist measurement changes that month.
