## 109. Starting Exercise With Excess Body Weight

### EN: Short
People of every body size can get stronger, fitter and healthier with the right kind of activity. A good start focuses on comfort, lower impact and being able to do more, and body weight is only one of several ways to measure success.

### EN: Detailed
**Choose accessible movement.** Walking, cycling, swimming, supported squats and machine strength exercises can be comfortable places to start, depending on your ability and preference. For some people, jogging and repeated jumping put more demand on the joints or breathing than low-impact options. Body size alone doesn’t make any of these activities right or wrong.

**Start with a dose you can do.** A short walk or a few sets of supported strength exercises may be enough to build the habit. Add time, distance or reps gradually as your confidence and tolerance grow. Pay attention to how movement feels and what you can do next week, and not only to the scale.

**Look at several results.** Easier stair climbing, longer walks, more reps and better recovery all count as progress. Changes in body weight depend on many factors, including eating patterns, medications and health conditions. Your fitness can improve without a big change in weight.

**Start where you are.** Some people already have plenty of exercise experience, while others have little equipment or privacy. Adjust exercises to your situation. Losing weight doesn’t have to be your goal.

**Using TrainUp.** In Profile, set your workout space, whether jumping is OK, any limitations by body area and your fitness level. The plan picks only exercises that fit those settings. TrainUp teaches exercise and doesn’t give diet plans or medical weight-management care, so if you have symptoms or complex health conditions, get individual advice from a doctor.
